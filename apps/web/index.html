A 2-MINUTE TEST

A quick test of how well you read client chats. Clients drop details that win deals, usually between the small talk. Most reps scroll right past them.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Choose your industry. You get 5 client chats, each with 5 messages.
  2. Tap every message with a detail that would help you close: something specific to this client, like what they love, their budget, a deadline, a worry or who else has a say.
  3. Skip the rest. Polite replies, scheduling and things every client says don't count, however good they sound.
  4. Every chat has at least one useful detail, and some have up to three. You won't know how many.
  5. One wrong tap ends that chat. We then show every detail you missed, and why each one matters.
One point per detail caught. Your score shows at the end.

Questions

Is it a CRM?

No. A CRM holds what you type into it. Tovira finds what your clients said that you didn't write down.

Do I have to connect my WhatsApp?

No. You export a chat yourself and send Tovira the file. It never touches your WhatsApp account.

Why not just ask ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers when you ask. Tovira files every detail under the right client, links it to the message, and brings it up before the meeting, without you asking.

Who is it not for?

Call-centre teams on recorded calls. Tovira is for people whose clients stay with them for years.
