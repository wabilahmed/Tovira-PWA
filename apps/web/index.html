Questions

Do I have to connect my WhatsApp?

No. WhatsApp has an Export Chat button; you send Tovira the file it produces. Tovira never connects to your account and cannot send messages as you.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes, including mixed with English, Hindi, and Urdu in the same sentence. That is what it was built for.

Is it a CRM?

No. A CRM is a form you fill in for your manager. Tovira is a memory that fills itself in for you. If you have a CRM, keep it.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

You can paste a thread into a chat window and get a decent answer. You cannot paste forty clients into it every morning, and it will never tell you on a Tuesday that a client has gone quiet.

What happens if I stop paying?

Export your entire book first. Nothing is held hostage.

Who is it not for?

Inside sales teams running recorded video calls. Good tools already exist for that. Tovira is for people who sell in person.
