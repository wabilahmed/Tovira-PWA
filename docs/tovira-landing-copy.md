## SECTION 3 — The problem, stated plainly

**Headline:**
Forgetting is silent.

**Body:**
You never feel the deal you lost to a forgotten promise. There is no moment
where it goes wrong — just a client who stops replying, and a reason you never
learn. Twenty relationships is already more than memory holds. Forty is not
close.

Your chat history has all of it. Nobody has time to scroll back through it.

## SECTION 4 — How it works (three steps, no icons)

**01 — Capture**
Talk. Thirty seconds in the car after a meeting is enough. Or paste a message,
or export a chat.

**02 — Tovira reads it**
Promises, people, dates, concerns, personal details — pulled out and filed
against the right client. Arabic, English, Hindi, Urdu — including all four in
one sentence.

**03 — It comes back when you need it**
A brief before the meeting. A statement on Monday. A note when a client goes
quiet. Tovira speaks rarely. When it speaks, act.

## SECTION 8 — Why not just ChatGPT

**Headline:**
ChatGPT waits to be asked.

**Body:**
You can paste a thread into a chat window and get a decent answer. You cannot
paste forty clients into it every morning, and it will never tap you on the
shoulder on a Tuesday to say that Falcon Group has gone quiet.

Tovira is not a place you go to think. It is the record that keeps itself, and
speaks up when something needs you.

## SECTION 11 — Questions

**Do I have to connect my WhatsApp?**
No. WhatsApp has an Export Chat button — you send Tovira the file it produces.
Tovira never connects to your account and cannot send messages as you.

**What if I stop paying?**
Export your entire book first. Nothing is held hostage.

**Does it work in Arabic?**
Yes — including mixed with English, Hindi, and Urdu in the same sentence. That
is what it was built for.

**Is it a CRM?**
No. A CRM is a form you fill in for your manager. Tovira is a memory that fills
itself in for you. If you have a CRM, keep it.

**Who is it not for?**
Inside sales teams running recorded video calls — those tools already exist and
they are good. Tovira is for people who sell in person.
