# Tovira

Tovira reads a sales rep's client chats and voice notes and catches the details that close deals.

## Who it is for

One problem, in two shapes: there is more to remember than a person can hold.

1. **Too long.** Relationship sellers whose clients stay for years. The detail that matters is buried in months of chat with one person.
2. **Too many.** High-volume sellers with more clients than anyone can keep in their head. They cannot recall who wanted what.

Both sell through conversation, and both lose deals to forgotten details. Write copy that either one recognises: say "your clients" and "your chats", not "long-term relationships" alone.

## The promise

**You walk into every meeting knowing what matters to this client.**

The feeling we sell is pride. A rep using Tovira looks sharp: they remember the sea view, the September deadline and who really makes the decision. Memory is how it works. Looking sharp is why they pay.

## What Tovira is not

- Not a CRM. A CRM holds what you type in. Tovira finds what the client said.
- Not a bot on the rep's WhatsApp. Reps send chat exports to Tovira's own WhatsApp number, or upload them. Tovira only receives. It never logs in to the rep's account, never sends messages as the rep and never messages their clients.
- Not an adviser. It states the detail. The rep decides what to do with it.

## Voice: the friendly coach

Tovira talks like a good coach who is on the rep's side. Warm, encouraging, brief. It notices what the rep did well and makes the next step easy.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Nice catch. She wants a sea view." | "DETAIL EXTRACTED: view preference." |
| "You promised the floor plan by Thursday. Want to send it now?" | "You have 1 overdue promise." |
| "She's been quiet for 16 days. Worth a hello?" | "Client at risk of churn." |
| "You've used this month's messages. You can add more in Settings." | "Limit exceeded." |

Rules that keep the coach from becoming annoying:

- One idea per sentence. Short words.
- Encourage, never scold. Tovira does not tell a rep what they missed as a failure; it tells them what it found.
- At most one exclamation mark on a screen, and none in errors.
- No jokes about the client. No emoji.
- A coach is honest. If Tovira is unsure, it says so. It never guesses a date or a name.

## Words

- **Messages, never money.** Allowances, limits and usage are always counted in messages. Currency appears only on the pricing section, the purchase screen and checkout.
- **No places.** Public copy names no country, city or district.
- **No people's names** in public copy. Say "a client", "she", "the buyer".
- Say **detail**, **promise**, **brief**, **going quiet**, **receipt**. Avoid "insight", "data point", "AI-powered", "leverage", "churn".
- Buttons say what happens: "Start free", "Send the chat", "Add messages".
- Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps except the small labels.

## Visual foundations: the Marker look

The idea: a printed transcript with one line marked by hand. Tovira's job is to mark the line that matters, so the brand's signature is a highlighter stroke.

- **A white page.** Background is `ground`. Cards are `surface` with a `line` border. No gradients, no glows, no dark mode on public pages.
- **Black does the work.** Headings, buttons and the logo are `ink`. The card that shows what Tovira caught is solid `ink` with `on-ink` text; it is the only dark block on a screen.
- **Yellow is the marker, and only the marker.** `highlight` appears in three places: on the client message Tovira caught, under the two or three key words of a headline, and as the bar of the logo. It is never a button, a background or a decoration. If it is on a screen more than twice, remove one.
- **The marked message tilts** by one degree, as if marked by hand. Nothing else is rotated.
- **One typeface.** Geist everywhere. Headings at weight 600 with tight letter spacing. Body at 400. Small labels are 12px, weight 600, uppercase, in `muted`. No serif and no monospace.
- **Shape.** Cards use `radius-lg`. Primary buttons are black pills. Chat bubbles have three round corners and one tight corner.
- **The signature image:** a client chat where one message is marked in yellow, next to the black card that shows what Tovira took from it.
- **Space.** Sections breathe: `space-8` on desktop, `space-7` on phones. Text blocks stay under about 65 characters wide.

## Logo

A black disc holding a letter T. The stem is white and the crossbar is a level yellow marker stroke that floats just above it. The word "Tovira" sits to its right in Geist 600, in `ink`.

- On a dark background, swap to a white disc with a black stem; the bar stays yellow.
- Keep clear space around it equal to half the disc.
- Never tilt the bar, recolour the disc or put the logo on a photo.
- The disc alone is the app icon and the browser tab icon.

## Iconography

Simple line icons: 2px stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in `ink` or `muted`. No filled icons, no emoji, no stock illustrations.

## Accessibility

- Text is at least 4.5:1 against its background. `muted` passes on `ground` and `surface`; on `surface-sunk` use `ink-soft`. Never put text in `highlight`; it only works as a fill behind `ink`.
- Touch targets are at least 44px tall.
- Motion is gentle and stops when the person asks for reduced motion.
